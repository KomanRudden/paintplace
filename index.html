About Us

Strijdom Park's paint destination

The Paint Place is a Dulux Specialist Paint Centre based in Strijdom Park, Johannesburg. We supply a comprehensive range of paint products and accessories to trade contractors and retail customers alike.

What sets us apart? We keep colour records on our computer system for every single colour that we tint — forever. Your custom shade from five years ago? We've got it on file, ready to mix again.

Our team is trained to advise on products and projects of any scale. Driven by honesty, expertise and customer satisfaction.
